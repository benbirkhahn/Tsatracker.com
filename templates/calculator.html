    Know what goes into your plan

    The line is only one part of the trip.

    A wait is an observation

    Official readings describe a particular checkpoint and lane at the time they were captured. They cannot predict the line for a future flight. This tool keeps your chosen security allowance and only increases it when a fresh reading is higher and your flight leaves within four hours.

    Make room for the rest

    Travel time is your estimate, without live traffic. Add time for parking, a shuttle or rental-car return, bag drop, and the walk to your gate. The gate buffer is how early you want to be at the gate before the flight departs. Check your airline’s exact deadlines, especially for checked bags and international travel.

    Check again before leaving

    If a reading is missing or old, your plan uses the security allowance you chose. That allowance is not a live or historical estimate. Confirm your terminal, checkpoint hours, and lane eligibility with your airport or airline before you travel. Leave extra time for assistance, document checks, or unfamiliar airports.
