What you'll be able to set

Alerts that respect your time.

01 / THRESHOLD

Over-the-line alert

Pick an airport and a number. If the standard or PreCheck wait crosses it, you hear about it — and not a minute before you need to.

02 / TREND

Rising-line alert

A low wait that's climbing fast is the one that catches people out. Get warned when the trend turns against you, not just when it's already bad.

03 / LEAVE-TIME

Time-to-go reminder

Tell us your flight. We combine the live wait, the recent trend, and the 30-day pattern, then nudge you when it's actually time to head for the airport.

04 / CHECKPOINT

Checkpoint-specific

At airports with terminal-level feeds, watch the exact checkpoint your trip uses instead of an airport-wide average.
